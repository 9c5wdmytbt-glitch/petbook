# 6. Proceed / change / stop gates, decision log and assumption register

## 6.1 Gates for releasing the US$15,000 product stage

These targets are **proposals calibrated to this test**, not industry benchmarks. Agree them **before** the interviews, so the results can't be argued away afterwards.

### PROCEED: all of the following are met

| # | Criterion | Target |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | Qualified interviews completed | 12 or more |
| G2 | Qualified interviewees confirming the pain (at least 2 hours from meeting end to approved draft, on their **last** meeting) | 8 or more of 12 |
| G3 | Of those, already recording meetings, or with a named person who can approve recording | 6 or more |
| G4 | Paid pilots signed **with cash received** at the US$300 floor or above | 3 or more |
| G5 | Repeated use: pilots submitting 2 or more meetings within 6 weeks | 2 or more of 3 |
| G6 | Measured correction time on our drafts against the customer's own baseline | Median cut of 50% or more |
| G7 | Critical errors (wrong decision, wrong vote, invented fact) reaching a customer-approved minute | Zero |
| G8 | A workable, documented route under the Data Protection Act, 2024 (local copy, transfers, processor terms) | Confirmed |
| G9 | Build quote for the focused product | Within US$15,000 for the agreed scope |

Note that G5 to G7 need pilot meetings, which may fall outside the 30 days. **Release the build money only after G5 to G7 are met. G1 to G4 alone only justify continuing the pilots.**

### CHANGE: the problem is real but the offer or buyer is wrong

| Signal | Likely change |
|---|---|
| Pain confirmed (G2) but fewer than 3 pilots, and the objection is price | Test the done-for-you per-meeting price, or a different buyer (in-house company secretaries at listed companies or parastatals) |
| Buyers' clients mostly already on board portals with AI minutes | Drop "board minutes", or sell as a quality/evidence layer for portal users. Probably a weaker business |
| Boards won't allow recording | Test notes-plus-agenda drafting with no audio, or stop |
| Minutes billed hourly, so saving time cuts revenue | Re-pitch as capacity (more clients per secretary) or target in-house teams |

### STOP: preserve the capital

| # | Trigger |
|---|---|
| S1 | Fewer than 5 of 12 qualified interviewees confirm the pain |
| S2 | No paid pilot after making 10 or more offers to hot or warm prospects |
| S3 | Most buyers say Copilot, Otter or a portal already does this "well enough" for no extra money |
| S4 | No lawful, affordable data-protection route |
| S5 | Corrected-time saving under 25% after 2 pilot meetings each |

**Stopping is a valid, successful outcome of validation.** It costs at most US$5,000 instead of US$30,000.
