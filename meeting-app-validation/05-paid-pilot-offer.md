**[VENTURE NAME] (PROPRIETARY) LIMITED**
**FOUNDING PILOT: EVIDENCE-LINKED BOARD MINUTES**
**OFFER TO [PRACTICE NAME]**

**1. WHAT WE ARE OFFERING**

1.1 For eight weeks from the start date, we will turn your recordings of formal board and committee meetings into draft minutes in your own template, with every decision, resolution and action linked to the moment in the recording that supports it.

1.2 This is a pilot of a service under development. During the pilot the work is done by software **and checked by a person**, as described in clause 3. It is not finished, self-service software.

**2. DELIVERABLES**

2.1 For each meeting submitted within the usage limits in clause 4, we will deliver:

2.1.1 draft minutes in Word (.docx), in your template, structured against your agenda;

2.1.2 an evidence-linked version, in which each material statement carries a reference to the transcript and the timestamp in the recording;

2.1.3 an action register showing each action, its owner, its due date and the source, with any owner or date that was inferred rather than stated clearly marked for your confirmation;

2.1.4 a list of reviewer flags: anything the recording does not establish, such as unclear audio, unconfirmed times, translations and possible name errors; and

2.1.5 the speaker-labelled, timestamped transcript.

2.2 We aim to deliver within two business days of receiving a usable recording and the agenda. This is a target, not a guaranteed service level.

2.3 We will not add attendance, votes, owners, deadlines or reasons that the recording and the agenda do not support.

**3. WHAT IS FUNCTIONAL, ASSISTED OR UNFINISHED**

| Capability | Status during the pilot |
|---|---|
| Transcription with speaker separation and timestamps | Functional, using a third-party speech-to-text service |
| Drafting minutes against the agenda | Assisted: AI draft, checked and corrected by our operator |
| Evidence links and reviewer flags | Assisted: produced by us, not yet automated end to end |
| Word output in your template | Assisted: set up by us at the start of the pilot |
| Customer log-in, online review and approval workflow | **Not available.** Delivery is by secure link or email, as agreed |
| Live recording, meeting bots and calendar integration | **Not available.** You supply the recording file |
| Setswana and mixed-language transcription | **Not supported.** Any non-English passage is flagged, not translated |

4.1 Up to **[6] meetings** during the pilot, with each recording no longer than **[3] hours**.

4.2 Recordings must be audio or video files in a common format. If a recording is unusable, we will tell you and it will not count towards the limit.

4.3 Additional meetings at **[US$100]** each, by agreement in writing.

5.1 You will make sure that meeting participants have been told that the meeting is recorded and that the recording will be processed by a service provider, and that you have the authority to share the recording with us.

5.2 You will supply the agenda and any previous minutes or template we need, and you will name one contact person.

5.3 You will review and approve all minutes before circulating them. The minutes remain your work product and your responsibility. We provide drafts, not legal advice or certification.

5.4 To help us measure the pilot, you will: (a) time your current minute-preparation process for at least one meeting; (b) record the time you spend correcting each of our drafts; and (c) take part in a 20-minute review at weeks 4 and 8.

## 5.A Price hypotheses to test (internal, do not send)

| Hypothesis | Figure | What would falsify it |
|---|---|---|
| H-P1 Pilot fee | US$600 for 8 weeks and 6 meetings (US$100 per meeting) | Most qualified, pain-confirmed buyers refuse at US$600 but would pay US$300. **Floor: US$300.** Below that, payment proves little |
| H-P2 Value per meeting | Saving at least 2 hours of secretary time per meeting is worth US$60 to US$120 | Buyers' stated cost of time × hours saved is below US$50 per meeting |
| H-P3 Subscription | US$249 per month per practice for up to 8 meetings | Buyers anchor below US$100 per month, or insist on per-meeting pricing below US$40 |
| H-P4 Billing model | Practices absorb minute-taking in retainers, so time saved is profit | Most bill minutes by the hour. Then the pitch must be capacity (more clients per secretary), not cost saving |

**Margin check:** at US$249 a month for 8 meetings, revenue is about US$31 per meeting. The handover's 20% variable-cost allowance is therefore about **US$6 per meeting**. After API costs (estimated at US$2 to US$4 for a 2-hour meeting, to be measured), that leaves **a few minutes** of paid operator time, not hours. So at that price, our own staff cannot review each meeting. That leaves three possibilities:

1. **The customer does the review** in our software. This is the product, and the assisted pilot is only a bridge to it.
2. **The price is much higher** for a "done-for-you" service. Test a done-for-you option at US$80 to US$150 per meeting as a separate hypothesis.
3. **Accept a lower margin** and revisit the ROI model.

Decide this from the pilot data, not now.
